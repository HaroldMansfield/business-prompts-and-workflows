# Business Prompts and Workflows

A free catalog of practical AI prompts and business workflows for founders, consultants, operators, and small business teams.

These workflows are designed to help people turn common business needs into structured AI-assisted work. Each entry includes a description, recommended use cases, the full prompt in an easy-to-copy code block, and usage tips where available.

## Who this is for

- Business owners who want practical AI workflows they can use right away
- Consultants and advisors who support clients with repeatable business processes
- Operators, marketers, and sales teams building better internal systems
- Professionals learning how to turn prompts into reusable AI skills

## Turning workflows into skills

Any workflow in this repository can be the starting point for a reusable AI skill. Start by creating a `SKILL.md` file, describe when the skill should be used, move the repeatable workflow instructions into the body, and place supporting templates or examples in dedicated folders.

See [Turn a Workflow Into a Skill](Skills/Turn-a-Workflow-Into-a-Skill.md) for a simple blueprint.
