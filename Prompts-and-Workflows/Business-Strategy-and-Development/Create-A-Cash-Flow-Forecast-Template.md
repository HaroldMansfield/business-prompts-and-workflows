# Create A Cash Flow Forecast Template

## Description

This prompt creates a Google Sheets Cash Flow Forecast Template that helps you track income, expenses, and cash flow projections using AI-powered calculations and logic.

## Best Use Cases

- Organizing business information into a structured working draft.
- Supporting planning, operations, or decision-making with guided questions.
- Creating a practical document that can be reviewed and adapted before use.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to build a simple but powerful cash flow forecast template in Google Sheets that helps small business owners forecast their cash flow for the next 12 months. The sheet should auto-calculate totals and flag cash flow issues.

User Questions (Gather Information):
1. What type of business is this (product-based, service-based, online)?
2. What are your regular monthly income sources?
3. What are your fixed monthly expenses?
4. What variable expenses (supplies, inventory, advertising) should we track?
5. How much starting cash do you have?
6. Do you need to project seasonal changes (high and low months)?

Template Build Instructions:

Create a Google Sheet with the following structure:
Income Tracker (monthly projected + actual)
Expense Tracker (fixed + variable + unexpected)
Cash Flow Calculation (monthly income - expenses)
Running Cash Balance (cumulative)
Automatic Alerts (highlight months where cash flow is negative)
```

## Usage Tips

- Build this into Google Sheets for real-time sharing and updating.
- Add chart visualizations for income vs. expenses over time.
- Use AI to auto-suggest categories based on business type.
- Update monthly to keep it useful.

## Review Notes

Review the output against your actual financial records and consult a qualified professional for tax, accounting, or financial decisions.
