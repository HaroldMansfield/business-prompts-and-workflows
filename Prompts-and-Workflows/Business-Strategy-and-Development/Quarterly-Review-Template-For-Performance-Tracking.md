# Quarterly Review Template (For Performance Tracking)

## Description

This workflow helps you create a structured Quarterly Business Review (QBR) that tracks your performance across goals, metrics, marketing efforts, sales, and team updates. It's ideal for internal review, team alignment, or investor reporting. turning raw activity into strategic insight.

## Best Use Cases

- Organizing business information into a structured working draft.
- Supporting planning, operations, or decision-making with guided questions.
- Creating a practical document that can be reviewed and adapted before use.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a Quarterly Business Review (QBR) document that summarizes company performance, highlights wins, and sets priorities for the next quarter.
Please ask me the following questions one at a time.
Once responses are gathered, generate a clear, structured QBR outline in plain text format.
1. What is your business name and what quarter is this review for? (e.g., Q1 2025)
2. What were your top 3-5 goals for this past quarter?
3. Which of those goals were achieved, in progress, or missed?
4. What key metrics or KPIs are you tracking? (e.g., revenue, traffic, MRR, churn rate)
5. What were your biggest wins or milestones this quarter?
6. What challenges or blockers did you face?
7. What did you learn or what insights stood out?
8. What are your top goals or priorities for the next quarter?
9. Are there any updates to your team, processes, or tools?
10. Do you want this review to include a SWOT summary or competitor snapshot?
Once complete, generate a Quarterly Business Review that includes:
- Quarter and review summary
- Goal tracking table (achieved/in progress/missed)
- KPI performance overview
- Highlights and milestones
- Challenges and takeaways
- Goals for next quarter
- Optional SWOT insights or competitor notes
Write the output in a format that can be copied into a presentation or Google Doc.
```

## Usage Tips

- Use at the end of each quarter to document wins, analyze performance gaps, and set new priorities.
- Great for solo founders, team leaders, or anyone needing a clean report for stakeholders.
- Combine with dashboards, spreadsheets, or metrics tools to enrich your report.
- Useful for strategic planning sessions or retrospectives.
- After generating your draft, edit or expand each section to reflect your actual data, KPIs, and priorities.

## Review Notes

Review the output carefully, adapt it to your business context, and verify important facts before sharing or using it.
