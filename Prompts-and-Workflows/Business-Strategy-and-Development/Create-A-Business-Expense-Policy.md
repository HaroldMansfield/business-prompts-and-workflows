# Create A Business Expense Policy

## Description

This prompt helps you generate a clear Business Expense Policy to guide employees, contractors, and founders on what expenses are covered, how to submit them, and reimbursement rules.

## Best Use Cases

- Organizing business information into a structured working draft.
- Supporting planning, operations, or decision-making with guided questions.
- Creating a practical document that can be reviewed and adapted before use.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to draft a clear, professional Business Expense Policy that defines allowable expenses, submission processes, and approval requirements.

User Questions (Gather Information):
1. What is your business name?
2. Who does this policy apply to (employees, contractors, owners)?
3. What types of expenses are reimbursable? (travel, meals, supplies, etc.)
4. What documentation is required (receipts, approval forms)?
5. What is the reimbursement timeline (within 30 days, etc.)?
6. Are there spending limits or pre-approval requirements?
7. Who approves expenses?
8. Should this policy cover both physical and virtual expenses (e.g., software subscriptions)?

Drafting Instructions:

Create a Business Expense Policy that includes:
Policy Scope & Purpose
Covered Expense Categories
Documentation & Submission Process
Approval Process & Limits
Reimbursement Timelines
Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Policy Updates & Contact Information
```

## Usage Tips

- Review quarterly to adapt to changing business needs.
- Store the policy in a shared team folder.
- Require pre-approvals for large or unusual expenses.
- Pair with an expense tracking app for easy management.

## Review Notes

Review the output against your actual financial records and consult a qualified professional for tax, accounting, or financial decisions.
