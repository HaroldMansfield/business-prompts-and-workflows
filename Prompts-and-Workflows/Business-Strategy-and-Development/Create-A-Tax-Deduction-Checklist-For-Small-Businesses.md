# Create A Tax Deduction Checklist For Small Businesses

## Description

This prompt creates a custom tax deduction checklist based on business type, helping you maximize deductions and stay compliant.

## Best Use Cases

- Organizing business information into a structured working draft.
- Supporting planning, operations, or decision-making with guided questions.
- Creating a practical document that can be reviewed and adapted before use.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to generate a tailored Tax Deduction Checklist to help the user maximize deductions and track deductible expenses throughout the year.

User Questions (Gather Information):
1. What type of business do you run?
2. Do you work from home?
3. Do you have employees or contractors?
4. What are your major expenses (rent, advertising, software, inventory, etc.)?
5. Do you travel for work?
6. Do you use your personal vehicle for business?
7. Do you offer benefits (health insurance, retirement contributions)?

Checklist Creation Instructions:

Create a Tax Deduction Checklist with these categories:
Home Office Deductions
Vehicle & Travel Deductions
Marketing & Advertising Costs
Professional Services (lawyers, consultants)
Technology & Software
Employee/Contractor Payments
Inventory & Supplies
Insurance & Benefits
Education & Training
Business Meals & Entertainment
```

## Usage Tips

- Review with a tax professional annually.
- Track expenses monthly to avoid last-minute tax prep stress.
- Customize for local tax laws (especially if international).

## Review Notes

Review the output against your actual financial records and consult a qualified professional for tax, accounting, or financial decisions.
