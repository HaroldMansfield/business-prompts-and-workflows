# Client Intake Form For Service Businesses

## Description

This workflow helps you create a Client Intake Form that collects essential information from new clients before beginning a service engagement. It captures client goals, preferences, relevant background, and any legal or operational disclaimers you need them to acknowledge. creating a professional first impression and protecting your business.

## Best Use Cases

- Organizing business information into a structured working draft.
- Supporting planning, operations, or decision-making with guided questions.
- Creating a practical document that can be reviewed and adapted before use.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a complete Client Intake Form that I can send to new clients or embed on my website. Please guide me through the following questions one at a time. After gathering my answers, generate a form with clearly labeled fields, grouped into logical sections.
1. What is your business name and what kind of services do you provide?
2. What is the purpose of this form? (e.g., general intake, onboarding, service prep)
3. What basic contact info do you want to collect? (e.g., name, email, phone, address)
4. What project or service-specific questions should be included? (e.g., goals, timeline, budget, pain points)
5. Do you need to collect information about previous service providers, platforms, or tools the client has used?
6. Should the form include scheduling preferences, availability, or preferred communication method?
7. Are there any required disclaimers, policies, or terms you want the client to acknowledge before proceeding?
8. Do you want the form to include file upload fields? (e.g., inspiration documents, contracts, brand assets)
9. Should the form include a digital signature or checkbox to confirm understanding/consent?
10. Will this be used as a Google Form, Typeform, web embed, PDF, or printed document?
Once complete, generate a clearly structured Client Intake Form that includes:
- Intro or welcome message
- Contact and business info
- Service-specific questions
- Expectations, goals, or customization preferences
- Scheduling or availability preferences
- Consent, terms acknowledgment, and liability disclaimer (if needed)
- Signature or agreement checkbox section
The tone should be professional but welcoming, with logical flow and accessible language.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for coaches, consultants, designers, agencies, therapists, wellness providers, and any service-based business.
- Customize this form by service type, or create multiple versions for different client segments.
- Use an online form builder (Google Forms, Jotform, Typeform) to make it easy to submit and manage.
- Include links to your Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and cancellation policy.
- Keep responses organized in a CRM or folder system to simplify onboarding and documentation.

## Review Notes

Review the output carefully, adapt it to your business context, and verify important facts before sharing or using it.
