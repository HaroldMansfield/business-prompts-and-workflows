# Self-Directed Learning Curriculum Generator

## Description

Generator Being an entrepreneur means constantly learning. This prompt creates a comprehensive, bootcamp- style learning curriculum for any skill or subject you want to master. The curriculum follows proven learning principles with a focus on high-impact content, practical application, and structured progression from fundamentals to advanced concepts.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating reusable guidance that can support multiple business functions.
- Building repeatable AI-assisted processes for communication, learning, or planning.
- Documenting useful context so future AI outputs are more consistent.

## Usage Tips

- Works will with ChatGPT Pro, Perplexity, and ClaudeAI.
- Be specific about your target skill or subject area.
- Specify any constraints (time available per week, existing knowledge, equipment limitations).
- Mention your learning style preferences if you have them.
- For technical or software-specific skills, ask for tutorials that use the latest versions.
- Request project ideas that align with your specific interests within the skill domain.
- Save the curriculum as a reference document and track your progress against it.
- Periodically request updates or modifications as your learning evolves.
- For proprietary systems or software, consider supplementing with official documentation.

## Review Notes

Review the output carefully, adapt it to your business context, and verify important facts before sharing or using it.
