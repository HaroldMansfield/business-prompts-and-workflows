# Brand Voice Builder

## Description

Use this workflow to train ChatGPT or any GPT on your brand’s tone and personality. Paste one step at a time, and let ChatGPT guide you.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating reusable guidance that can support multiple business functions.
- Building repeatable AI-assisted processes for communication, learning, or planning.
- Documenting useful context so future AI outputs are more consistent.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Use this workflow to train ChatGPT or any GPT on your brand’s tone and personality. Paste one step at a time, and let ChatGPT guide you.

-----

Step 1: Audience Snapshot
"Let’s start with your audience. Please answer these questions:

1. Who is your ideal customer? (Age, job, pain points) 
2. What are they trying to achieve? 
3. What frustrates them or gets in their way? 
4. What kind of tone or language do they typically respond to?"

(Wait for user response)

-----

Step 2: Brand Personality
"Great. Now let’s define your brand’s voice like it’s a person.

1. What 3, 5 words describe your brand’s personality? (e.g., warm, witty, bold) 
2. How should your brand *sound* in writing? 
3. What should your brand *never* sound like?"

(Wait for user response)

-----

Step 3: Voice Rules & Style Guide
"Let’s lock in your voice rules.

1. Any words or phrases your brand uses a lot? 
2. Any words you avoid? 
3. Sentence style: Short & punchy? Long & detailed? 
4. Do you use emojis, humor, or casual expressions?"

(Wait for user response)

-----

Step 4: Writing Samples (Optional)
"Last step! Paste 2, 3 samples of things you’ve already written in your brand voice (or type a quick intro paragraph about your business). I’ll use these as references when generating content."

-----

💡 Tip:
Once complete, save your answers to a doc and upload it to your GPTs in the future. or paste into new prompts to keep your tone consistent.
```

## Usage Tips

- Answer the prompt questions with specific details from your business.
- Treat the AI output as a working draft and revise it before using it.
- Save the final version somewhere your team can find and reuse it.

## Review Notes

Review the output carefully, adapt it to your business context, and verify important facts before sharing or using it.
