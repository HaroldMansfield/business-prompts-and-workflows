# Social Media Posting Plan (AI-Generated Captions, Hashtags, and Schedules)

## Description

This workflow helps you generate a complete social media content plan, including AI-written captions, suggested hashtags, and a consistent posting schedule. It ensures your content aligns with your marketing goals, brand voice, and platform best practices. saving time while maintaining engagement.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a Social Media Posting Plan, including ready-to-publish captions, hashtag recommendations, and a weekly schedule.
Please ask the following questions one at a time.
Once complete, generate a platform-specific content plan with posting ideas.
1. What is your business name and what do you offer?
2. What are your primary marketing goals? (e.g., awareness, conversions, traffic, community building)
3. What social media platforms do you want to focus on? (e.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, X/Twitter, Pinterest)
4. What is your preferred posting frequency per platform?
5. What is your brand voice or tone? (e.g., educational, witty, inspirational, direct)
6. What are your core content pillars or themes? (e.g., tips, behind-the-scenes, testimonials, promotions)
7. Do you want captions to include a call-to-action (CTA)? If yes, what kind?
8. Are there upcoming dates, events, or launches to include in the plan?
9. Do you want hashtag suggestions? If so, how many per platform?
10. Would you like suggested times/days for optimal posting?
Once all answers are collected, generate a Social Media Posting Plan that includes:
- Weekly or monthly calendar layout
- Post topics organized by platform and date
- AI-written captions customized per post and tone
- Hashtag sets (3-10) per post, based on platform
- CTA suggestions and emoji usage (if applicable)
- Posting schedule with suggested times per platform
Deliver the content in a structured format, ideal for copy-pasting into a scheduler or spreadsheet.
```

## Usage Tips

- Use this workflow monthly or quarterly to plan ahead and batch content.
- Works well with content scheduling tools (e.g., Buffer, Later, Metricool, Hootsuite).
- Pair with your Content Calendar Generator for long-term strategy alignment.
- Repurpose high-performing content across platforms with slight caption edits.
- Track post engagement and adjust topics or tone as needed.
- Review and edit or fine-tune the generated message to align with your brand voice and audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
