# Client Proposal Template

## Description

This workflow helps you create a professional client proposal that clearly outlines your services, pricing, timeline, and scope of work. It's ideal for service providers, freelancers, consultants, and agencies who need to present structured, compelling offers that win business and reduce back-and-forth.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a polished, persuasive client proposal tailored to my services and the prospect's needs. Please guide me through the following questions one by one. Once I've answered, generate a complete proposal draft that I can edit and send to the client.
1. What is your business name and what type of services do you provide?
2. Who is the client or company this proposal is for?
3. What services or solutions are you proposing?
4. What is the problem the client is facing, and how does your solution help?
5. What is the scope of work? (Include deliverables, format, and quantity if applicable)
6. What is the timeline for delivery or milestones?
7. What are your rates or pricing for this project? (Flat fee, hourly, package, retainer)
8. Are there any assumptions, limitations, or exclusions the client should be aware of?
9. What are the payment terms? (e.g., deposit required, net 15, staged payments)
10. Do you want to include testimonials, previous results, or case studies?
11. What call to action or next step should the client take if they accept the proposal?
After collecting answers, write a client proposal that includes:
- Cover page with business name and client name
- Executive summary of the problem and solution
- Project scope and deliverables
- Timeline and milestones
- Investment and pricing section
- Terms and conditions or disclaimers
- About your business or credentials (optional)
- Call to action and contact information
Use professional, persuasive language that's easy to read and looks great when pasted into a branded PDF or Google Doc.
```

## Usage Tips

- Works best for service-based proposals (e.g., design, marketing, coaching, consulting).
- Customize the tone for corporate, creative, or startup clients.
- Use this template with tools like PandaDoc, Canva, or Google Docs for branded delivery.
- Always include next steps or expiration dates to drive action.
- Pair with the Sales Script Generator to align your pitch and proposal.
- Review and edit or fine-tune the generated message to align with your brand voice and audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
