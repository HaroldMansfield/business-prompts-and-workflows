# Affiliate Recruitment Email Sequence

## Description

This workflow helps you generate a multi-step affiliate recruitment email sequence designed to attract and onboard new partners into your affiliate program. It includes an initial outreach message, a follow-up reminder, and a final call-to-action email. crafted to highlight your offer, benefits, and ease of participation.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me generate a 3-part affiliate recruitment email sequence that introduces my program and encourages qualified individuals to sign up.
Please ask me the following questions one at a time.
Once complete, write all three emails in a persuasive, brand-appropriate tone.
1. What is your business name and what products/services do you offer?
2. What is your affiliate program about? (Brief description)
3. What benefits or commissions do affiliates receive? (e.g., % of sales, recurring payments, performance bonuses)
4. Who is your ideal affiliate partner? (e.g., influencers, coaches, past customers, niche bloggers)
5. What makes your affiliate program appealing or unique?
6. What's the signup process? (e.g., quick form, invite-only, review required)
7. Do you want the tone to be professional, conversational, or enthusiastic?
8. What is the link or landing page affiliates should visit?
Once complete, generate a 3-part email sequence:
- Email 1: Initial outreach (introduce the offer and benefits, invite to join)
- Email 2: Reminder (follow up with additional benefits or social proof)
- Email 3: Final call to action (urgency, last chance, recap of why it's a good fit) Each email should include a clear CTA and a signup link.
```

## Usage Tips

- Best used when launching or expanding an affiliate program in eCommerce, SaaS, or content-based businesses.
- Use for both cold outreach (to potential affiliates) and warm outreach (to past customers or existing influencers).
- Customize for different affiliate types: content creators, bloggers, coaches, niche experts, or brand fans.
- Add links to your affiliate program page, terms, or signup form.
- Track responses using your email or CRM platform to optimize send timing.
- Be sure to edit or fine-tune the generated emails for tone, brand language, and your specific audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
