# Sales Script Generator (for Calls, Pitches, and Follow-Ups)

## Description

This workflow generates a custom sales script designed to help you confidently guide conversations with leads. whether on discovery calls, product demos, or follow-ups. It tailors messaging to your audience, product, and offer, while helping you handle objections and guide the conversation toward conversion.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me write a customized sales script for use in sales calls, discovery calls, pitches, or follow-up conversations. Please guide me through the following questions one at a time. Once the responses are complete, generate a structured script I can follow or adapt.
1. What is your business name and what product or service are you selling?
2. Who is your ideal customer or target audience?
3. What is the primary goal of this conversation? (e.g., schedule a demo, close a sale, qualify a lead)
4. What makes your product or service valuable or unique?
5. What are the most common objections you hear from leads?
6. What kind of call is this for? (cold call, warm lead, referral, inbound inquiry, follow-up)
7. What tone or approach should the script take? (e.g., casual, consultative, confident, high-energy)
8. Is there a specific structure you prefer? (e.g., question-led, value pitch, storytelling)
9. What's the ideal outcome or next step you want to close with? (e.g., book a call, send a proposal, take payment)
Once complete, generate a full sales script that includes:
- A warm introduction and context setting
- Key discovery questions to understand the lead's needs
- Value-driven pitch tailored to pain points
- Responses to common objections
- A clear call to action or next step
- Optional follow-up email or message template
Use persuasive, natural language and organize the script into a flow that's easy to follow during live calls.
```

## Usage Tips

- Customize for different lead types (cold, warm, referral) or industries.
- Practice using the script out loud and adapt your tone for live conversations.
- Use the objection-handling section to prepare for tough questions.
- Pair with CRM tools or note-taking apps to track how scripts perform.
- Revisit and refine monthly based on conversion feedback and results.
- Review and edit or fine-tune the generated message to align with your brand voice and audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
