# Use NotebookLM To Analyze Competitor Websites

## Description

competitor websites Helps you perform a detailed competitor analysis of websites in your industry to identify opportunities for improving your own website's positioning and messaging. This analysis can be completed in minutes rather than the hours it would take to compile manually.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Setup Instructions
1. Create a new notebook in NotebookLM titled "Competitive Research" (or name of your choice)
2. Add competitor homepage URLs as sources in your notebook
3. Use the prompts below to analyze the data
Prompt 1: Competitor Analysis
Act as an experienced marketing researcher Function as a digital market intelligence analyst
specializing in [insert your industry or niche]. Examine the collection of competitor homepage
URLs in this notebook and deliver a comprehensive assessment covering:
What recurring value propositions and key messaging themes emerge across these
websites?
Which specific services or offerings receive prominent placement across multiple
competitors?
Based on language, design, and content focus, who appears to be the intended audience
segment for these businesses?
What communication style and brand voice patterns are dominant in this competitive
landscape?
Analyze the social proof elements (testimonials, case studies, client logos, reviews) used by
competitors
Evaluate content strategies including blog topics, publication frequency, and content
formats
Identify apparent weaknesses or gaps in competitor offerings that represent opportunities
Compare pricing strategies, models, and transparency across competitors
Prompt 2: Website Gap and Opportunity Analysis
Next, conduct an evaluation of my website [insert your website URL]. Drawing from your
competitive landscape findings, please identify:
3-5 Strategic Positioning Variances: How my digital presence differs significantly from
industry norms
3-5 Market Differentiation Pathways: Unique elements or approaches my site could
emphasize to create competitive advantage
Specific optimization recommendations to better connect with the same customer
demographic served by competitors
Content strategy opportunities based on competitor gaps or underserved topics
User experience improvements compared to competitor sites
Trust-building element recommendations based on effective competitor approaches
Potential pricing or offer structure adjustments to improve competitive positioning
```

## Usage Tips

- Save each analysis as a separate document for future reference.
- Focus on 3-5 top competitors for the most relevant insights.
- Update your analysis quarterly or whenever you notice significant changes in your industry.
- Use the findings to inform your website updates, content strategy, and marketing messaging.
- This technique works well for analyzing other marketing materials (social media, emails, etc.).

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
