# Content Calendar Generator

## Description

This workflow generates a strategic Content Calendar customized to your business goals, audience, and marketing channels. It outlines what to post, when, and where. helping you maintain consistency across platforms, plan ahead for key dates, and align your content with promotions, launches, or seasonal themes.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me build a 30- to 90-day content calendar for my business.
Please ask me the following questions one at a time.
Once the answers are collected, generate a detailed content calendar that includes post types, topics, platforms, and recommended posting frequency.
1. What is your business name and what do you offer?
2. What are your marketing goals for this time period? (e.g., brand awareness, sales, email list growth)
3. Who is your target audience and what kind of content do they prefer?
4. What platforms do you want to use? (e.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, blog, YouTube, email)
5. How often do you want to post on each platform?
6. Are there any specific promotions, events, launches, or campaigns coming up?
7. Do you have specific themes or content pillars you want to cover? (e.g., tips, testimonials, behind-the-scenes)
8. Are there any brand voice or style guidelines to follow?
9. Do you already have any existing content that can be repurposed?
10. Would you like your calendar to include national holidays or seasonal tie-ins?
Once the answers are provided, generate a content calendar that includes:
- A date-by-date plan with post topics
- Platform assignments (what goes where)
- Suggested formats (e.g., video, carousel, blog, email)
- Key themes, CTAs, and content goals
- Notes for batching, scheduling, or outsourcing content
Present the calendar in an easy-to-follow format, organized by week or platform.
```

## Usage Tips

- Best used for 30-, 60-, or 90-day planning cycles.
- Works well with social scheduling tools (e.g., Buffer, Later, Hootsuite).
- Update or refresh monthly to keep content relevant and timely.
- Repurpose high-performing content across different platforms.
- Use alongside the Social Media Posting Plan prompt for captions and hashtags.
- Review and edit or fine-tune the generated message to align with your brand voice and audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
