# Marketing Plan (AI-Powered Framework)

## Description

This workflow helps you create a customized, strategic Marketing Plan tailored to your business goals, target audience, and industry. By guiding you through key questions, it produces a comprehensive, actionable plan that includes goals, audience insights, channels, messaging, and KPIs. all informed by AI prompts and structured frameworks.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me generate a full marketing plan for my business. Please guide me through the following questions one by one. Once all inputs are collected, generate a clear and practical plan that I can use for execution and tracking.
1. What is the name of your business and what do you offer?
2. What are your short-term and long-term business goals?
3. Who is your target audience? (Include demographic and psychographic details)
4. What makes your product/service unique or valuable?
5. Who are your main competitors?
6. What marketing efforts have you tried before? What worked or didn't?
7. What is your marketing budget or resource availability?
8. What platforms or channels do you want to prioritize? (e.g., email, social media, search, events)
9. Do you have a sales funnel or customer journey defined?
10. How will you define success? What are your key performance indicators (KPIs)?
11. What tools or software are you using to manage your marketing?
Once these questions are answered, generate a structured marketing plan that includes:
- Business overview and marketing objectives
- Audience segments and messaging strategy
- Core marketing channels and recommended tactics
- Monthly or quarterly campaign themes
- Suggested tools and platforms
- Budget guidelines and content formats
- KPIs and measurement plan
- Recommendations for testing, iteration, and optimization Use a professional and easy-to-understand tone that makes the plan actionable and adaptable.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for early-stage startups or solo founders planning their first campaigns.
- Update this plan quarterly to align with new goals or market conditions.
- Use the KPIs section to track campaign effectiveness and adapt over time. This prompt works well with ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity when paired with prior research. Save and share the final plan with your team, freelancers, or marketing partners.
- Review and edit or fine-tune the generated message to align with your brand voice and audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
