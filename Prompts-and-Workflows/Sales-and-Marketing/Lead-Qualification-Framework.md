# Lead Qualification Framework

## Description

This workflow helps you build a Lead Qualification Framework that identifies which leads are worth pursuing based on their fit, intent, and potential value. It uses AI to guide you through defining your ideal customer profile (ICP), buying signals, and scoring criteria. so your sales process becomes more efficient and effective.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me define a custom Lead Qualification Framework that I can use to score and prioritize leads. Please walk me through the following questions one at a time. Once all responses are collected, generate a lead scoring model and qualification checklist.
1. What is your business name and what do you offer?
2. What types of customers are the best fit for your business? (Think industry, company size, budget, role, etc.)
3. What problems do your ideal customers typically face?
4. What are the key buying signals that indicate a lead is ready to engage or purchase?
5. What behaviors show that a lead is not a good fit? (e.g., no budget, wrong industry, not decision-makers)
6. What sources do most of your high-converting leads come from? (e.g., referrals, LinkedIn, cold outreach, website forms)
7. What is your sales cycle length or process? (e.g., discovery call > proposal > close)
8. Do you want to use a scoring system (e.g., 1-10, A/B/C rating, weighted points)?
Once complete, create a Lead Qualification Framework that includes:
- An ideal customer profile (ICP)
- Qualification questions to ask during discovery
- Lead scoring criteria based on fit and interest
- A checklist for sales team or CRM input
- A simple rating or tiering system (e.g., hot/warm/cold or 1-100 score)
- Tips for disqualifying or nurturing unqualified leads
Use a practical, structured format that can be adapted into CRM tags or sales playbooks.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for sales teams, solo founders, or service providers working with inbound or outbound leads.
- Pair this with your CRM (e.g., HubSpot, Pipedrive, Notion) for lead tracking. Helps prioritize follow-ups and avoid wasting time on low-potential leads.
- Review and revise criteria quarterly based on close rates and trends.
- Can be adapted for both B2B and B2C sales workflows.
- Review and edit or fine-tune the generated message to align with your brand voice and audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
