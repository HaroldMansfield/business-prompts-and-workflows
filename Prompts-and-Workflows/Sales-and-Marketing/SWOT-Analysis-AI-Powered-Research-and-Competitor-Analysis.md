# SWOT Analysis (AI-Powered Research and Competitor Analysis)

## Description

This workflow helps you generate a comprehensive SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) for your business or a specific product/service. The prompt is designed to combine internal insights with external market research. helping you make informed strategic decisions and identify growth opportunities.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a comprehensive SWOT analysis for my business, combining internal insights with external market and competitor research. Ask me the following questions one at a time.
Once complete, generate a SWOT analysis with clear bullet points under each category.
1. What is the name of your business and what do you offer?
2. What are your key internal strengths? (e.g., team skills, brand reputation, IP, customer loyalty)
3. What are your internal weaknesses or areas for improvement? (e.g., budget, tech stack, process gaps)
4. Who are your main competitors and how do you compare to them?
5. What trends or external opportunities are emerging in your industry? (e.g., technology, regulation, audience behavior)
6. What external threats or risks are you concerned about? (e.g., competitors, market downturns, supply chain issues)
7. Do you want to focus this SWOT on your overall business, a product/service, or a specific campaign?
Once answers are provided, generate a SWOT analysis that includes:
- Strengths: Internal capabilities and advantages
- Weaknesses: Internal challenges or limitations
- Opportunities: External trends, gaps, or innovations you can capitalize on
- Threats: External risks, competition, or market barriers
Format the output in a clean 2x2 matrix or as four labeled sections with clear, actionable bullet points.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for quarterly reviews, investor presentations, new product launches, or marketing pivots.
- Pair this with competitor research tools (e.g., Perplexity, ChatGPT with web browsing, NotebookLM) to enhance the Opportunities and Threats sections.
- Use this as a collaborative workshop activity with your team or leadership group.
- Be honest and objective when inputting internal factors4this improves strategic clarity.
- After the analysis is generated, review and refine the output to ensure accuracy, context, and relevance to your business goals.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
