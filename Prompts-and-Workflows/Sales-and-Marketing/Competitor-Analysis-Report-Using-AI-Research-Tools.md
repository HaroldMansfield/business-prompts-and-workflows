# Competitor Analysis Report (Using AI Research Tools)

## Description

This workflow helps you generate a detailed Competitor Analysis Report by leveraging AI tools like Perplexity, ChatGPT with browsing, or NotebookLM. It guides you through collecting relevant data about your competitors. such as pricing, positioning, content strategy, and product features. so you can better differentiate your business and identify market opportunities.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a Competitor Analysis Report for my business.
Please ask me the following questions one at a time, and once complete, generate a formatted report comparing me to my competitors.
1. What is your business name and what do you offer?
2. What are the top 2-4 businesses you consider competitors? Please provide names and websites if available.
3. What specific areas do you want to analyze? (e.g., pricing, branding, product features, customer reviews, SEO, social media presence)
4. What is the goal of this analysis? (e.g., improve positioning, develop new features, refine pricing, identify content gaps)
5. Do you want a side-by-side comparison table in the final output?
6. Do you have any public tools you use for research (e.g., Perplexity, Ahrefs, Semrush, SimilarWeb)?
Once complete, generate a competitor analysis that includes:
- Brief profiles of each competitor
- Feature, pricing, or offer comparisons
- Marketing and messaging strategy breakdown
- Social media activity and engagement levels
- Customer sentiment insights (based on reviews/testimonials)
- SEO or traffic overview if data is provided
- A summary of insights, gaps, and opportunities for your brand
Format the report for easy scanning — use headings, bullet points, and optional comparison tables.
```

## Usage Tips

- Use this workflow when launching a new product, preparing a marketing strategy, or planning investor materials.
- Enhance results by combining AI-generated insights with manual research (e.g., reviewing websites, social media, reviews).
- Organize outputs into a presentation, document, or spreadsheet for internal use.
- You can repeat the prompt for multiple competitors or compare side-by-side.
- Be sure to review and refine all AI-generated insights for accuracy and relevance before using them in decision-making.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
