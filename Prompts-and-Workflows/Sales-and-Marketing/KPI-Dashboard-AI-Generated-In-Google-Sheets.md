# KPI Dashboard (AI-Generated In Google Sheets)

## Description

This workflow helps you generate a custom KPI dashboard structure that tracks your most important business metrics using Google Sheets. It guides you in selecting the right performance indicators, organizing your data inputs, and structuring the layout for ongoing visibility into growth and performance.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me structure a performance tracking dashboard using Google Sheets. Please walk me through the following questions one at a time.
Once complete, generate a layout plan I can build or copy into a spreadsheet.
1. What is your business name and what industry are you in?
2. What type of metrics do you want to track? (e.g., marketing, sales, operations, customer success)
3. What are your top 5-10 KPIs? (e.g., website traffic, conversion rate, revenue, churn, cost per lead)
4. How frequently do you want to update the data? (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly)
5. Do you want to track actuals vs. goals or trends over time?
6. Do you prefer viewing metrics in tables, charts, or both?
7. Do you have multiple teams, products, or departments to track separately?
8. Will this be used solo or shared with a team?
Once answers are provided, generate a dashboard outline with:
- Sheet tabs by category (e.g., Overview, Sales, Marketing, Operations)
- A metrics table for each tab with columns for KPI name, data source, current value, goal, and notes
- Suggested visualizations (e.g., bar charts, trend lines, pie charts)
- Recommendations for color coding or alerts
- Instructions for linking to Google Analytics, CRM, or manual entry if needed
Write the final output as a clean outline that can be copy-pasted into a blank Google Sheet or shared with a team.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for founders, managers, and teams tracking sales, marketing, operations, or customer metrics.
- Use this as a starting template. then link it to live data sources or update it weekly/monthly.
- Customize the dashboard based on your business model (e.g., SaaS, service, eCommerce, coaching).
- Use conditional formatting and charts in Google Sheets to make your KPIs visually intuitive.
- After generating the outline, edit and tailor each section to match your tools, tracking methods, and business needs.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
