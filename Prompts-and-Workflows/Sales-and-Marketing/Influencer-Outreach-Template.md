# Influencer Outreach Template

## Description

This workflow helps you generate a personalized influencer outreach message that's professional, compelling, and aligned with your brand goals. It guides you in crafting an email or DM that builds relationships with influencers and encourages collaboration. whether for product gifting, paid partnerships, or affiliate promotion.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a personalized influencer outreach message that I can send via email or direct message. Please guide me through the following questions one at a time.
Once complete, generate the full message in a professional yet friendly tone.
1. What is your business name and what do you sell or promote?
2. What is the goal of this collaboration? (e.g., product seeding, sponsored post, affiliate partnership)
3. What type of influencer are you targeting? (e.g., industry, audience size, engagement style)
4. Do you have a specific influencer in mind? If so, share their name or handle.
5. What makes your product or brand appealing to influencers?
6. What are you offering in exchange for the collaboration? (e.g., free product, flat fee, commission, exclusive perks)
7. Are there specific deliverables or content types you expect from them?
8. What's the ideal next step for them to take after receiving the message?
Once all answers are collected, generate a professional outreach message that includes:
- Personalized greeting and intro
- Brief pitch of your brand and product
- Reason for the outreach (why them?)
- What's being offered and what you're asking for
- Clear next step (e.g., reply, book a call, click a link) Write it in a tone appropriate for the selected platform (email or DM) and format it for easy copy and paste.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for influencer campaigns in eCommerce, lifestyle, coaching, or wellness.
- Customize the tone for cold outreach vs. warm intros.
- Research each influencer beforehand to tailor your message authentically.
- Include your offer, value proposition, and expectations upfront.
- Always include a call-to-action (CTA), such as scheduling a call or replying for more info.
- Review and edit or fine-tune the generated message to align with your brand voice and audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
