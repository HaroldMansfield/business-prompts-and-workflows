# Business Model Canvas (AI-Guided Completion)

## Description

This workflow helps you fill out a complete Business Model Canvas, the popular one-page framework used to define your business model clearly and concisely. It covers all nine essential sections. from customer segments to revenue streams. using guided prompts that turn abstract ideas into actionable insights.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me complete a full Business Model Canvas for my business. Please walk me through the following nine sections with targeted questions. Once we've completed all parts, generate a clearly organized summary of the canvas in plain text or table format.
1. What is the name of your business and what product/service do you offer?
Now ask for the following Business Model Canvas sections one at a time:
2. Customer Segments — Who are your target customers? Are there different types or segments?
3. Value Propositions — What problems are you solving or value are you creating for each customer segment?
4. Channels — How do you deliver your product/service to customers? (e.g., online, retail, partners)
5. Customer Relationships — What kind of relationship do you want to maintain? (e.g., self-service, personal, community)
6. Revenue Streams — How does your business make money? (e.g., subscriptions, licensing, one-time purchases)
7. Key Resources — What assets are critical to your business success? (e.g., IP, staff, technology, funding)
8. Key Activities — What do you need to do extremely well? (e.g., manufacturing, marketing, delivery, customer service)
9. Key Partnerships — Who do you work with to deliver your value? (e.g., suppliers, distributors, affiliates)
10. Cost Structure — What are the biggest costs or expenses? (e.g., labor, software, manufacturing, advertising)
Once all questions are answered, format the results clearly with each of the nine sections labeled and summarized.
Use concise and business-friendly language that makes it easy to visualize and share.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for founders validating an idea, pivoting an existing business, or preparing for fundraising.
- Great for use in team strategy sessions or startup accelerators.
- You can use this to create one canvas per product or market segment if needed.
- Once complete, transfer the results to a visual template or whiteboard for clarity.
- Be sure to review and adjust each section as needed based on real customer data or evolving business strategy.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
