# Customer Journey Mapping Tool

## Description

This workflow helps you create a Customer Journey Map. a visual and strategic breakdown of how prospects interact with your brand from first awareness to post-purchase. It helps you identify opportunities, content gaps, and touchpoints to improve marketing, sales, onboarding, and customer retention.

## Best Use Cases

- Planning a sales, marketing, content, or growth activity with clearer inputs.
- Creating a working draft that can be reviewed, edited, and shared with a team or contractor.
- Turning scattered business context into a more structured execution plan.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a full Customer Journey Map for my business. Please walk me through the following questions one at a time. After collecting my answers, generate a stage-by-stage journey map including touchpoints, pain points, and opportunities.
1. What is your business name and what do you offer?
2. Who is your ideal customer or audience?
3. What is the main product or service you want to map?
4. What is the typical sales cycle or funnel? (e.g., ad → lead magnet → consult → close)
5. What are the primary marketing channels or touchpoints? (e.g., website, social media, email, ads)
6. What tools or platforms are used in your marketing and customer service stack?
7. What are the key customer goals at each stage? (e.g., research, compare, decide, succeed)
8. What pain points or drop-offs occur at different stages of the customer experience?
9. What actions do you want the customer to take at each stage?
10. What internal teams are involved at each stage? (e.g., marketing, sales, onboarding, support)
Once complete, generate a Customer Journey Map that includes:
- Stages (e.g., Awareness, Consideration, Decision, Onboarding, Retention, Referral)
- Customer actions and mindset at each stage
- Key brand touchpoints (ads, email, call, demo, etc.)
- Pain points or common drop-off areas
- Opportunities to improve or automate experiences
- Metrics to track success across the journey
Present the results in an outline format that can be adapted into a visual map or slide deck.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for improving sales processes, client onboarding, or customer retention strategies.
- Use in team strategy meetings to align marketing, sales, and support roles.
- Adapt for both digital and physical businesses (eCommerce, service, SaaS, etc.).
- Pair with automation tools to streamline touchpoints (e.g., email, CRM, retargeting).
- Review this quarterly to update based on changes in audience behavior or funnel performance.
- Review and edit or fine-tune the generated message to align with your brand voice and audience.

## Review Notes

Before using the final output, check that the messaging, assumptions, timelines, and recommended channels match your audience, brand voice, and available resources.
