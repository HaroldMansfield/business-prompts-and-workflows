# Influencer Collaboration Agreement

## Description

This workflow generates a legally sound Influencer Collaboration Agreement that defines the partnership between a business and an influencer or content creator. It outlines campaign details, deliverables, compensation, content usage rights, timelines, disclosure compliance, and termination terms to ensure clarity and protect both parties.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me draft a complete Influencer Collaboration Agreement that protects both my business and the influencer. Please ask me the following questions one at a time, then generate a full agreement once all information is collected.
1. What is your business name and contact information?
2. What is the full name, social handle(s), and contact information of the influencer?
3. What is the purpose of the collaboration? (e.g., promote a product, attend an event, create sponsored content)
4. What deliverables will the influencer provide? (e.g., Instagram posts, TikTok videos, blog reviews)
5. What platforms will the content be posted on?
6. What are the deadlines or posting schedule?
7. What is the compensation structure? (e.g., flat fee, per post, affiliate commission, product trade)
8. Will you provide any products, services, or materials for the campaign?
9. Will your business have rights to reuse the content? (e.g., for ads, reposting, email marketing)
10. Are there any brand guidelines or messaging notes the influencer must follow?
11. Are there specific hashtags, tags, or disclosure statements required for FTC compliance?
12. What is the duration of the agreement and can it be extended?
13. What happens if either party wants to terminate the agreement early?
14. In what jurisdiction should the agreement be governed?
Once complete, draft a professional Influencer Agreement that includes:
- Identification of both parties
- Description of the campaign, services, and deliverables
- Timeline and posting schedule
- Compensation and payment terms
- Usage rights and intellectual property provisions
- Brand compliance and disclosure guidelines
- Confidentiality and exclusivity clauses (if applicable)
- Termination and dispute resolution terms
- Governing law and signature section
Ensure the tone is professional and clear, using legal language appropriate for brand partnerships.
```

## Usage Tips

- Use for any paid or unpaid collaborations to define expectations upfront.
- Include FTC disclosure rules (e.g., ad, #sponsored) to remain compliant.
- Clarify whether you can repurpose the content in ads, emails, or your own social media.
- Consider exclusivity terms if you want the influencer to avoid promoting competitors.
- Review this agreement with new influencers or agencies before each campaign.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
