# Disclaimers For Content Creators, Coaches, and Consultants

## Description

This workflow creates a tailored Disclaimer Statement that protects your business from liability related to advice, opinions, or outcomes shared via your website, blog, courses, social media, or coaching sessions. It sets clear boundaries on what your audience can expect, especially around financial, health, legal, or personal results.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me write a legally protective and clearly worded disclaimer for my business.
Please ask me the following questions one at a time. Once all information is collected, draft a professional disclaimer that can be added to a website, podcast, video description, course platform, or email footer.
1. What is the name of your business or personal brand?
2. What type of content, services, or advice do you offer? (e.g., health tips, business coaching, investment education)
3. Which platforms do you publish content on? (e.g., website, YouTube, Instagram, email newsletter)
4. Do you offer any free or paid downloadable resources (e.g., guides, templates, worksheets)?
5. Are you certified or licensed in any professional field? (e.g., nutritionist, attorney, CPA) If not, do you want to clarify that?
6. What results do your audience members or clients often expect? (e.g., weight loss, income growth, legal protection)
7. What potential risks, misunderstandings, or misuses are you trying to protect yourself against?
8. Do you want to include a liability waiver or limitations of responsibility clause?
9. Should the disclaimer include an affiliate disclosure if you promote products or tools?
10. In what legal jurisdiction should the disclaimer apply?
Once you've provided answers, generate a full disclaimer that includes:
- A general liability disclaimer for the information provided
- Clarification that results may vary and are not guaranteed
- Statement that no legal, medical, or financial advice is being given (if applicable)
- Disclosure of affiliate relationships (if applicable)
- Copyright or intellectual property disclaimer (if needed)
- Jurisdiction and legal protections
- Placement guidance (website footer, course intro, etc.)
The tone should be clear, legally cautious, and accessible to the general public.
```

## Usage Tips

- Use this on any platform where you share guidance or opinions that could be interpreted as professional advice.
- Add to your website's footer, FAQ page, or directly below blog posts, videos, and digital products.
- Tailor separate disclaimers for high-risk industries like finance, legal, wellness, or fitness.
- If you promote affiliate products or use sponsorships, include an FTC-compliant disclosure.
- Review this statement regularly as your business services or content evolves.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
