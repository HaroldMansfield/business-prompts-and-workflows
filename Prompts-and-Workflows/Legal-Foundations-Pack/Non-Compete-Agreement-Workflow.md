# Non-Compete Agreement Workflow

## Description

This workflow creates a legally tailored Non-Compete Agreement that prevents employees, contractors, or partners from working with competitors or starting a competing business within a specified time, region, or industry. It's designed to protect your intellectual property, trade secrets, and client relationships after someone leaves your business.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a Non-Compete Agreement that protects my business from unfair competition. Please walk me through the following questions one at a time, and once all information is gathered, draft a complete agreement using professional legal language that's still easy to understand:
1. What is your business name and address?
2. Who is this agreement for? (Full name of the employee, contractor, or partner)
3. What is the person's role or relationship to your business?
4. What specific information or assets are you trying to protect? (e.g., customer lists, processes, proprietary methods, IP)
5. Which geographic region will this agreement cover? (e.g., city, state, national, global)
6. What is the duration of the restriction? (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, 2 years)
7. What types of competitive activity are restricted? (e.g., working for a competitor, starting a similar business, soliciting clients)
8. Are there any industries or job types exempt from this agreement?
9. What happens if the agreement is violated? (e.g., legal action, damages, injunction)
10. In what state/country should this agreement be governed?
11. Do you want the agreement to include a non-solicitation clause (preventing poaching of clients/employees)?
12. Would you like this to be mutual (applies to both parties) or one-sided?
After collecting all answers, write a complete Non-Compete Agreement that includes:
- Agreement title and effective date
- Parties involved and purpose
- Definitions of competitive activity and protected interests
- Time, geographic, and industry limitations
- Legal remedies and enforcement options
- Signature block for both parties
The tone should be formal, precise, and legally enforceable. 1—
```

## Usage Tips

- Best used when hiring contractors, employees, or collaborators with access to proprietary business data.
- Be specific about time, geography, and competitive scope to avoid overly broad terms that might not be enforceable.
- Always check enforceability in your local jurisdiction. some states (like California) limit or prohibit non-competes.
- Use a lawyer to review the final version if your business operates across multiple states or industries.
- Combine with a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) for stronger protection if needed

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
