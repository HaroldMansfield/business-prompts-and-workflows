# Create A Client Service Agreement

## Description

This prompt generates a Client Service Agreement for businesses that provide services to clients. It defines the scope of services, payment terms, responsibilities, and expectations to protect both parties and reduce legal risk.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your goal is to create a contract that protects the business while clearly setting client expectations.

User Questions (Gather Information):

Ask the user:
1. What is the business name and client name?
2. What specific services will you provide to the client?
3. What are the project timelines, milestones, or deadlines?
4. What is the pricing model? (flat fee, hourly rate, retainer)
5. When and how will payments be made? (upfront deposit, installment payments, final payment)
6. What happens if the client cancels or changes the scope?
7. What happens if either party wants to terminate the contract early?
8. What are the client's responsibilities (providing info, access, approvals)?
9. Will you provide any warranties or guarantees?
10. In what jurisdiction should the agreement be governed?

Agreement Drafting Instructions:

Draft a complete Client Service Agreement including:
Business and client details
Description of services provided
Project scope and timeline
Payment terms and schedule
Change requests and scope creep policy
Cancellation and termination terms
Client responsibilities (e.g., providing timely feedback)
Dispute resolution process
Governing law and jurisdiction
Signature and date fields
```

## Usage Tips

- Use for every new client. even if they are friends or family.
- Customize the scope and pricing for each project.
- Add clear language around scope creep to protect against endless revisions.
- Require upfront deposits to secure bookings.
- Keep a signed copy for your records and provide one to the client.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
