# Supplier Agreement For Vendors and Wholesalers

## Description

This workflow generates a customized Supplier Agreement that outlines the terms between your business and a vendor or wholesaler. It covers product specifications, delivery timelines, payment terms, quality standards, and remedies. ensuring clear expectations and protecting both parties in case of issues.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a professional Supplier Agreement that clearly defines expectations and responsibilities. Please guide me through the following questions one at a time. Once complete, draft a full agreement using clear legal language suitable for B2B relationships.
1. What is your business name, address, and contact information?
2. What is the supplier's legal name and address?
3. What goods or products will the supplier provide?
4. Are there specific product standards, specs, or quality requirements?
5. What are the delivery terms and timelines? (e.g., weekly, monthly, on-demand)
6. What is the agreed pricing structure? (e.g., per unit, bulk discounts, tiered rates)
7. What are the payment terms? (e.g., 50% upfront, net 30, on delivery)
8. What are your expectations for packaging, labeling, or branding?
9. What happens if products are defective or delivered late?
10. Are there penalties for breach or failure to deliver?
11. Is there a return or refund policy? If so, under what conditions?
12. What is the term of this agreement and how can it be terminated?
13. In what legal jurisdiction should this agreement be governed?
Once all information is gathered, write a complete Supplier Agreement that includes:
- Parties and purpose
- Product descriptions and quality expectations
- Order process, delivery schedule, and shipping terms
- Payment structure and invoicing terms
- Inspection, acceptance, and return terms
- Liability, indemnification, and remedies
- Confidentiality clause (if applicable)
- Dispute resolution and governing law
- Agreement duration and termination clauses
- Signature section
Use professional and legally clear language suitable for small business transactions.
```

## Usage Tips

- Ideal for businesses buying physical inventory from manufacturers, wholesalers, or artisans.
- Be detailed on product specs and deadlines to avoid disputes.
- Use for both domestic and international suppliers, and adjust terms accordingly.
- Always clarify payment terms to avoid late payment issues.
- Review this agreement annually or whenever suppliers change their policies or pricing

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
