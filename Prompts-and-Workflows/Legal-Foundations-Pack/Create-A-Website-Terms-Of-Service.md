# Create A Website Terms Of Service

## Description

This prompt helps business owners generate a custom Terms of Service (TOS) that outlines how visitors and customers can use their website, purchase products, and interact with the business. It reduces legal risk, manages customer expectations, and protects intellectual property.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
User Questions (Gather Information):

Ask the user the following:
1. What is your business name, website URL, and official business address?
2. What products, services, or content do you offer on the website?
3. Do you sell physical products, digital products, services, or all three?
4. Do you have specific age restrictions (e.g., must be 18+ to purchase)?
5. Do you allow user-generated content (comments, reviews, forums)?
6. What payment methods do you accept? (Credit cards, PayPal, crypto, etc.)
7. What is your refund/return/cancellation policy?
8. Do you offer subscriptions or recurring billing?
9. What countries do you serve?
10. What law governs these terms (your business location, or international law)?
11. How can customers contact you for disputes or legal issues?

TOS Creation Instructions:
Once the questions are answered, draft a clear and comprehensive Terms of Service document.
Cover key sections: Introduction, Acceptance of Terms, Products & Services Description, User
Responsibilities, Payment Terms, Refund/Return Policy, Intellectual Property, Dispute Resolution, Liability
Limitation, and Governing Law.
Use professional, plain language — legal but not confusing.
Ensure terms align with actual business practices (e.g., refunds, warranties).

Optional:
Add a "Prohibited Activities" section to cover misuse, spam, and illegal activity.
Create a short version for easy reading, with a link to the full document.
```

## Usage Tips

- Make sure your TOS matches your actual business operations. don't copy-paste from unrelated templates.
- If you serve multiple countries, clarify which laws apply (home country vs. international regulations).
- Consider adding an arbitration clause to reduce costly lawsuits.
- Place a clear link in your website footer, next to Privacy Policy.
- Review and update TOS regularly, especially if you add new services or products.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
