# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) - GDPR Compliance

## Description

This workflow creates a custom Data Processing Agreement (DPA) designed to meet GDPR compliance when you're sharing user or customer data with third-party processors (e.g., cloud platforms, marketing tools, freelancers). It outlines responsibilities for data protection, breach handling, and compliance. protecting both your business and your users.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me draft a legally sound and GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreement (DPA) between my business (the "Data Controller") and a third-party service provider (the "Data Processor").
Please ask me the following questions one at a time to gather all necessary details. Once complete, generate the full DPA in a professional legal format:
1. What is the full legal name and address of your business (Data Controller)?
2. What is the full name and address of the third-party (Data Processor)?
3. What kind of personal data will be processed? (e.g., names, emails, IP addresses, payment info)
4. Who are the data subjects? (e.g., customers, employees, subscribers)
5. What is the purpose of data processing?
6. What services is the processor providing that require access to this data?
7. Where will the data be processed? (Specify if data is transferred outside the EU/EEA)
8. What security measures are in place to protect the data? (e.g., encryption, access controls)
9. What is the duration of the agreement and data retention terms?
10. What is the protocol in case of a data breach?
11. Can the processor hire sub-processors? If so, under what conditions?
12. What are the rights and responsibilities of each party under GDPR?
13. In what jurisdiction should the DPA be legally enforced?
Once you've gathered all the details, write a complete Data Processing Agreement that includes:
- Introduction of parties and definitions
- Purpose and subject of processing
- Type of data and categories of data subjects
- Duration and termination terms
- Rights and obligations of both controller and processor
- Data security and confidentiality clauses
- Data breach notification process
- Sub-processor use and accountability
- International data transfer safeguards (if applicable)
- Governing law and jurisdiction
- Signature block for both parties
Ensure the language is GDPR-compliant, precise, and suitable for international agreements.
```

## Usage Tips

- Required if you work with any external service that handles personal data on your behalf (e.g., CRM, email platforms, analytics).
- Applies even if you're based outside the EU but have EU-based users/customers.
- Keep this on file with each third-party processor and review annually.
- For tools like Stripe, Google, or Mailchimp, check if they offer their own standard DPA and compare terms.
- Pair this with your Privacy Policy to demonstrate full compliance in case of audits or disputes.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
