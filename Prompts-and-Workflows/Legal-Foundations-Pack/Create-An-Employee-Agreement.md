# Create An Employee Agreement

## Description

This prompt generates a comprehensive Employee Agreement that defines the rights, responsibilities, compensation, and policies for new hires. It ensures clear expectations between employer and employee, protects business interests, and helps with compliance.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your goal is to draft a clear, professional agreement tailored to the business's needs and compliant with applicable employment laws.

User Questions (Gather Information):

Ask the user:
1. What is the business name, address, and contact information?
2. What is the employee's full name, position, and start date?
3. Is the position full-time, part-time, or temporary?
4. What is the salary, hourly rate, or compensation structure?
5. How often will the employee be paid? (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
6. Will the employee receive benefits? (healthcare, PTO, retirement plan)
7. What are the employee's key responsibilities?
8. Will there be a probationary period? If so, how long?
9. What is the termination policy? (notice period, severance, grounds for termination)
10. Will the employee have access to confidential information? If so, include a confidentiality clause.
11. Should the agreement include a non-compete or non-solicitation clause?
12. In what jurisdiction should this agreement be governed (state/country)?

Agreement Drafting Instructions:

Draft a comprehensive Employee Agreement that includes:
Business and employee details
Job title, description, and responsibilities
Compensation and benefits
Work schedule and location (on-site, remote, hybrid)
Performance expectations and evaluation process
Confidentiality and intellectual property clauses
Probationary period (if applicable)
Termination and resignation process
Non-compete and non-solicitation clauses (if requested)
Governing law and dispute resolution process
Signature block for employer and employee
```

## Usage Tips

- Always customize for each role. don't rely on generic templates.
- Make sure the compensation and benefits match your actual policies.
- Review with an employment lawyer to ensure compliance with labor laws in your state or country.
- Keep a signed copy in the employee's personnel file.
- Update agreements if roles, pay, or policies change over time.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
