# Product Liability Waiver For Health, Beauty, Fitness, Or Risk-Based Products

## Description

This prompt generates a Product Liability Waiver designed to limit your business's legal responsibility for potential injuries, allergic reactions, side effects, or misuse of your physical products. especially in industries like health, wellness, fitness, skincare, and supplements. It helps protect your business from claims related to use or results of your products.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a clear and enforceable Product Liability Waiver. Please ask the following questions one at a time. Once all details are collected, draft a complete waiver appropriate for consumer use.
1. What is your business name and contact information?
2. What type of product(s) does your business sell? (e.g., skincare, supplements, fitness gear)
3. What potential risks or side effects should customers be made aware of?
4. Are there specific instructions or warnings that users must follow?
5. Are your products approved or regulated by any official body? (e.g., FDA, CE, Health Canada)
6. Do you want to include language stating that results are not guaranteed?
7. Do you sell to minors or require age restrictions?
8. Will this waiver be presented at checkout, within packaging, or before product use?
9. Do you want to include a clause requiring users to consult a doctor before use? (common for supplements or fitness products)
10. In what jurisdiction should this waiver be governed?
After gathering answers, write a legally sound Product Liability Waiver that includes:
- Business and product identification
- Acknowledgment of risks, side effects, or misuse
- User responsibility to follow usage instructions
- Non-guarantee of outcomes or results
- Disclaimers for minors or those with medical conditions
- Hold harmless clause to release your business from liability
- Governing law and legal venue
- Signature/acknowledgment language (or checkbox notice for digital use) Use accessible, professional language that informs the customer and limits business liability.
```

## Usage Tips

- Especially important if you sell anything applied to the body or consumed, or that could cause injury.
- Include this as a click-to-agree checkbox at checkout, or insert in product packaging with clear language.
- Add a reminder to consult a professional before using health- or fitness-related products.
- Keep it updated as your product line or legal environment evolves.
- For digital-only purchases, include language in both the Terms of Service and order confirmation page.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
