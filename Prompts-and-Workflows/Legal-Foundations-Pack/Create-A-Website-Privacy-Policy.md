# Create A Website Privacy Policy

## Description

This prompt helps business owners create a professional Privacy Policy for their website. It ensures the policy covers data collection, storage, processing, and user rights. including GDPR compliance for businesses that serve EU customers. By answering a series of tailored questions, you can generate a complete, customized Privacy Policy ready for immediate use.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to create a complete, legally compliant Privacy Policy tailored to my business website. Please ask me the following questions one at a time, and once you have all the answers, draft a complete Privacy Policy that meets general legal standards (including GDPR if applicable). The policy should be clear, professional, and easy for customers to understand.

Questions to Ask You:
1. What is the official name of your business?
2. What is your business website URL?
3. What types of data do you collect from users (e.g., name, email, phone, payment info, IP address, cookies)?
4. How do you collect this data (e.g., contact forms, cookies, newsletter signups, purchases)?
5. Why do you collect this data (e.g., marketing, analytics, order fulfillment, legal compliance)?
6. How long do you retain customer data?
Do you share this data with third parties (e.g., analytics tools, payment processors, shipping partners)? If
7. so, which ones?
8. Do you sell customer data to any third parties?
9. Do you operate in or sell to customers in the EU? (If yes, GDPR compliance is required)
10. How can users contact you to request data deletion or access to their data?
11. Do you use cookies or tracking technologies? If so, what type (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel)?
12. What legal jurisdiction governs your Privacy Policy (e.g., US, EU, specific state like California)?
Do you want to include a section on children's data (e.g., if you operate a website directed at children under
13. 13)?

After gathering all answers, generate a complete Privacy Policy that includes:
Introduction and business identity
Types of data collected
Methods of collection
Purposes of data use
Third-party sharing and disclosures
Data retention periods
User rights (access, correction, deletion, portability)
Cookie and tracking policy
GDPR-specific rights (if applicable)
Contact details for data inquiries
Jurisdiction and governing law
Last updated date.
```

## Usage Tips

- Tailor for Your Industry: If you operate in health, finance, or other regulated industries, include any industry- specific data rules.
- Update Regularly: Laws change, so review this policy every 6-12 months.
- GDPR Matters Even Outside the EU: If your site gets visitors from Europe, GDPR applies. even if you're a US business.
- Link to Your Privacy Policy Clearly: Add a visible link in your footer and during any data collection (forms, checkouts).
- Consider Legal Review: AI can save you time and give you a solid foundation, but having a lawyer review your final document (especially if you handle sensitive data) is a smart move.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
