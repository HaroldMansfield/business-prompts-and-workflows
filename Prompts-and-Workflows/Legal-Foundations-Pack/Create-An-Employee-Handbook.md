# Create An Employee Handbook

## Description

This workflow helps you generate a custom Employee Handbook that outlines your company's: Culture and values Expectations and policies Code of conduct Working hours Benefits Leave policies Workplace safety This comprehensive guide gives new hires a clear understanding of what to expect and how to succeed in your organization.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me generate a complete and professional Employee Handbook for my company. Please guide me through a series of questions to customize it. Once all answers are collected, write the full handbook.
Start by asking me these questions, one at a time:
- What is your company's name and brief mission statement?
- How would you describe your company culture and core values?
- What is your workplace setup? (Remote, in-office, hybrid)
- What are your standard working hours and break policies?
- What holidays do you observe and what is your PTO/vacation policy?
- What types of employees do you hire? (Full-time, part-time, freelancers)
- What benefits do you offer? (Health insurance, —01k, wellness programs, etc.)
- What's your dress code policy?
- What's your code of conduct? (e.g., professionalism, inclusivity, respect)
- What is your policy on harassment and workplace safety?
- What tools or platforms do employees use? (e.g., Slack, Zoom, company laptop)
- Do you have policies on internet use, device use, or confidentiality?
- What is the disciplinary and termination process?
- Any industry-specific policies or sections you'd like to include?
Employee Handbook Components:
Once all answers are provided, draft a complete Employee Handbook that includes:
- Welcome message and mission. Introduction to the company and its purpose.
- Company culture and values. The principles that guide my organization.
- Work schedule and attendance. Expectations for hours, breaks, and time off.
- Compensation and benefits overview. Details on pay, insurance, and other perks.
- Conduct policies. Guidelines for professional behavior.
- Leave policies. Vacation, sick leave, and other time off.
- Workplace safety. Procedures for maintaining a safe environment.
- IT and communication policies. Rules for using company technology.
- Disciplinary procedures. Steps taken when policies are violated.
- Legal disclaimer and acknowledgment section. Formal recognition of handbook receipt
The tone should be friendly, clear, and professionally—written so employees understand expectations and feel empowered from day one.
```

## Usage Tips

- Best practices for implementing your employee handbook: Best for startups hiring their first few team members, or businesses formalizing culture and operations.
- You can customize different versions for contractors vs employees if needed.
- Works well in Notion, Google Docs, or as a downloadable PDF.
- Add your company logo and branding before sharing it with staff.
- Review it quarterly as policies evolve. and have an HR/legal pro glance at it if needed.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
