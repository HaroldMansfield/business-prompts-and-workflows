# Create An LLC Operating Agreement

## Description

This workflow helps you generate a customized LLC Operating Agreement that clearly outlines the roles, responsibilities, ownership percentages, and decision-making processes for your Limited Liability Company. By answering guided questions, you'll walk away with a complete, legally sound agreement tailored to your specific business and member structure.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me generate a legally sound and customized LLC Operating Agreement. Please guide me through a series of questions one at a time. Once you've gathered all necessary information, draft the full agreement.
Start by asking:
- What is the full legal name of your LLC?
- In which U.S. state is the LLC being formed?
- Will the LLC be member-managed or manager-managed?
- Please list the full names and addresses of each member.
- What percentage of ownership does each member hold?
- What is the initial capital contribution (if any) for each member?
- How will voting rights be assigned? (Equal per member or based on ownership?)
- How will profits and losses be distributed?
- What are the rules for adding or removing a member?
- What happens if a member wants to leave, dies, or becomes incapacitated?
- What is the duration of the LLC? (e.g., perpetual or fixed term)
- Will there be any restrictions on transferring ownership?
- In case of disputes, what is the resolution method? (e.g., mediation, arbitration, court)
- Any other provisions you'd like to include?
LLC Operating Agreement Components:
Once all answers are provided, draft the LLC Operating Agreement using clear, professional legal language. Include:
Key Components:
- Title, Date, and Member List - The formal introduction to your agreement with all parties clearly identified
- Management Structure and Member Roles - Clearly defined responsibilities and decision-making authority
- Voting and Profit-sharing Rules - How decisions are made and how financial benefits are distributed
- Transfer, Buyout, and Dissolution Clauses - Procedures for ownership changes and business closure
- Signatures Section - Space for all members to formally agree to the terms
- Ensure the output is legally structured but easy for non-lawyers to understand.
```

## Usage Tips

- Use this workflow before registering your LLC to clarify internal roles. or after registration to formalize things.
- For single-member LLCs, the output will simplify accordingly.
- Review the final agreement with a legal professional to ensure it aligns with your state's laws.
- Store a signed copy on a NAS, secure drive, and/or in a secure cloud folder and share with all members.
- Revisit the agreement annually or whenever your business structure changes.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
