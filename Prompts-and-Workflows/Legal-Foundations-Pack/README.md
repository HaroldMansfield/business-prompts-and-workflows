# Legal Foundations Pack

Workflows that help draft business legal documents, policies, agreements, and compliance-related materials.

## Legal disclaimer

These workflows are educational drafting aids, not legal advice. They may help you organize requirements and create a first draft, but they do not replace a qualified attorney. Laws vary by jurisdiction and change over time. Have any legal, employment, privacy, compliance, or contractual document reviewed by an appropriate professional before using it with customers, employees, contractors, partners, or vendors.
