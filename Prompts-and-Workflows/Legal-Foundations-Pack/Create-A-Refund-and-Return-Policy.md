# Create A Refund and Return Policy

## Description

This prompt generates a clear, customer-friendly Refund & Return Policy tailored to your business type, products, and industry. It sets clear expectations, reduces disputes, and helps ensure legal compliance with consumer protection laws.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to create a Refund & Return Policy that protects the business while ensuring clear and fair terms for customers.

User Questions (Gather Information):

Ask the user:
1. What is your business name, website URL, and contact information?
2. What types of products or services do you sell? (physical goods, digital products, services)
3. What is your timeframe for returns or refund requests? (30 days, 14 days, etc.)
4. What are your requirements for returns? (original packaging, unused, proof of purchase)
5. Do you offer exchanges, store credit, or full refunds?
6. Who pays for return shipping (customer or business)?
7. Are there non-refundable items (e.g., custom orders, digital downloads)?
8. What is the process for requesting a refund or return? (form submission, email contact, portal)
9. Are there any restocking fees or processing fees?
10. Do you ship internationally? If so, do policies differ for international orders?

Policy Drafting Instructions:

Draft a Refund & Return Policy that includes:
Overview of the policy
Timeframe for returns/refunds
Eligibility criteria (condition, packaging, proof of purchase)
Process for initiating a return/refund
Refund methods (original payment method, store credit, exchange)
Non-refundable items list (if applicable)
Shipping and restocking fee policies
Special rules for international orders (if relevant)
Legal disclaimer and governing law (if needed)
```

## Usage Tips

- Make sure the policy aligns with your actual operational capabilities. don't promise refunds faster than you can process them.
- Use plain language so customers easily understand.
- Post the policy on your website footer, checkout page, and in order confirmation emails.
- Review local consumer protection laws to ensure compliance.
- For digital products, clarify if refunds are allowed (many jurisdictions have different rules).

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
