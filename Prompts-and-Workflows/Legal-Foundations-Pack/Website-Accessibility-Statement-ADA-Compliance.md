# Website Accessibility Statement (ADA Compliance)

## Description

This workflow generates a Website Accessibility Statement that communicates your business's commitment to making its website usable for individuals with disabilities. It outlines accessibility standards, tools used, and contact info for support. helping protect you under ADA, WCAG, and other international web accessibility regulations.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me write a clear and professional Accessibility Statement for my business website that communicates our efforts to support users with disabilities and ensure compliance with accessibility regulations.
Please ask me the following questions one at a time, and then generate the full statement based on my answers:
1. What is the name of your business?
2. What is your business website URL?
3. What accessibility standards do you aim to meet? (e.g., WCAG 2.1 Level AA, ADA, Section 508)
4. Have you used any specific accessibility tools or audits? (e.g., WAVE, UserWay, AccessiBe, manual testing)
5. Are there areas of your website currently being improved for accessibility?
6. Who can users contact if they encounter accessibility barriers? (Provide name, email, phone if available)
7. Do you want to include a commitment to continuous improvement or accessibility roadmap?
8. Should the tone be formal and legal, or friendly and approachable?
9. In what jurisdiction should the statement be applicable?
Once you've gathered the answers, draft a complete Accessibility Statement that includes:
- An opening declaration of commitment to accessibility
- Mention of standards followed (WCAG/ADA/etc.)
- Accessibility tools or methods used for testing and compliance
- Areas currently under review or improvement
- Contact information for reporting accessibility issues
- Acknowledgment of ongoing efforts and future improvements
- Date of last update
- Optional: jurisdiction and governing law reference
The statement should be simple, clear, and easy to locate on a website.
```

## Usage Tips

- Essential for any business website, especially in the U.S., Canada, UK, and EU.
- Even if you're not fully compliant yet, stating your commitment and contact info can reduce legal risk.
- Include a "Report an Issue" contact and monitor for feedback.
- Pair this with regular audits or accessibility plugin use to improve UX for all users.
- Add the finalized statement to your website footer or Accessibility page and keep it updated every 6, 12 months.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
