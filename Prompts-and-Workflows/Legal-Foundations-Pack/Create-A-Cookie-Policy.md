# Create A Cookie Policy

## Description

This prompt generates a legally-compliant Cookie Policy for business websites that use tracking technologies. It helps businesses meet privacy regulations like GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, and CCPA, while also educating visitors on how cookies are used.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to draft a clear, comprehensive Cookie Policy that meets the requirements of GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, and other relevant laws.

User Questions (Gather Information):

Ask the user:
1. What is your business name, website URL, and contact information?
2. What types of cookies does your website use? (essential, functional, analytics, advertising, social media)
3. What are the purposes for using cookies? (site functionality, user analytics, personalized ads)
4. Does your site use third-party cookies (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel)?
5. Do you offer visitors the ability to accept, reject, or customize cookie preferences?
6. Do you serve customers in the European Union (where GDPR applies)?
7. Do you serve customers in California, USA (where CCPA applies)?
8. How long are cookies stored (session vs. persistent cookies)?
9. How can users change their cookie preferences later? (link to preference center)
10. In what jurisdiction should the policy be governed?

Policy Drafting Instructions:

Draft a complete Cookie Policy including:
Introduction explaining what cookies are
Types of cookies used (essential, functional, analytics, advertising, social media)
Purposes of each cookie type
Explanation of first-party vs. third-party cookies
Cookie duration and storage periods
How users can manage their preferences
Links to third-party cookie policies (if applicable)
Legal disclosures for GDPR, CCPA, and other relevant laws
Contact information for privacy questions
Date of last update
```

## Usage Tips

- Link the Cookie Policy directly from your website footer and your cookie banner.
- If operating in the EU, implement a Consent Management Platform (CMP) to collect and record consent.
- Use clear, non-technical language so visitors understand their choices.
- Periodically audit your site to ensure your Cookie Policy reflects actual technologies in use.
- Consider combining the Cookie Policy into your broader Privacy Policy if that fits your website structure.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
