# Licensing Agreement For Licensing Your Intellectual Property

## Description

This workflow creates a Licensing Agreement that allows your business to legally grant permission to another party to use your intellectual property. such as brand assets, content, software, training materials, or inventions. under defined terms. It covers usage rights, fees, duration, exclusivity, and legal protections to help monetize your IP while maintaining control.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me create a complete Licensing Agreement that grants legal permission to another party to use my intellectual property under specific terms. Please walk me through the following questions one at a time. Once complete, draft a full agreement using professional legal formatting.
1. What is your full legal name (or business name) and address?
2. What is the full name and address of the licensee (the party receiving the license)?
3. What type of intellectual property are you licensing? (e.g., logo, brand, software, course materials, product design)
4. Are you the sole owner of this IP, or are there co-owners?
5. Will the license be exclusive, non-exclusive, or limited by territory or industry?
6. What are the allowed uses of the licensed material? (e.g., marketing, resale, incorporation into products)
7. Are there any restrictions on use? (e.g., cannot modify, cannot sublicense, must include attribution)
8. What is the duration of the license? (e.g., 1 year, perpetual, renewable annually)
9. What is the licensing fee or royalty structure? (flat fee, monthly, percentage of revenue, tiered model)
10. What happens if the licensee violates the agreement?
11. Do you want the agreement to include confidentiality clauses or non-disclosure terms?
12. In what jurisdiction should the agreement be governed?
Once all answers are provided, generate a complete Licensing Agreement that includes:
- Introduction and identification of parties
- Description of licensed intellectual property
- Scope of license and usage rights
- Term, renewal, and termination clauses
- Compensation structure and payment terms
- Restrictions and limitations
- Confidentiality and attribution requirements
- Legal remedies for breach
- Governing law and dispute resolution
- Signature block for both parties
Use professional legal language, while keeping it understandable for non-lawyers. 2—
```

## Usage Tips

- Useful when sharing brand materials, resell rights, white-label content, or course licenses.
- Always clarify whether the license is exclusive, non-exclusive, or limited to a region or industry.
- For recurring fees or royalties, specify payment terms and audit rights.
- Consider including watermark or attribution requirements for brand protection.
- Consult an IP attorney if licensing patents, complex software, or high-value IP internationally.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
