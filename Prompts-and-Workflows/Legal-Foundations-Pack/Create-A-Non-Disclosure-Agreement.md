# Create A Non Disclosure Agreement

## Description

This prompt helps you generate a custom, legally-sound Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) to protect confidential business information when sharing it with partners, contractors, employees, investors, or collaborators. This ensures sensitive information. such as business plans, financial data, intellectual property, or trade secrets 4 remains protected.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your goal is to draft a clear, enforceable NDA tailored to the user's specific situation.

User Questions (Gather Information):

Ask the user the following questions to customize the NDA:
1. What is your business name, address, and primary point of contact?
2. Who will sign this NDA? (individual, company, contractor, partner, investor, employee)
What type of confidential information will be shared? (business plans, financial data, product designs,
3. customer lists, etc.)
In what context will the information be shared? (potential partnership, investment pitch, hiring process,
4. vendor collaboration, etc.)
Is this a one-way NDA (only one party shares information) or a mutual NDA (both parties exchange
5. confidential information)?
How long should the NDA remain in effect? (common terms: 2-5 years or until the information becomes
6. public)
7. In which country/state should the NDA be legally enforceable?
8. What are the penalties or remedies if the receiving party violates the NDA?
Should the NDA include any exclusions (e.g., publicly known info, independently developed info, legally
9. required disclosures)?

NDA Creation Instructions:
Once these questions are answered, draft a complete and legally compliant NDA. The document should

include:
Title: Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
Effective Date and Parties Involved
Definition of Confidential Information
Obligations of Receiving Party
Permitted Uses of Confidential Information
Duration of Confidentiality Obligation
Exclusions from Confidentiality (e.g., public information)
Remedies for Breach (injunctive relief, damages, etc.)
Governing Law and Jurisdiction
Signature Block (with space for authorized signatures and dates)

Tone:
Use clear, professional, legally precise language.
Avoid unnecessary legal jargon to keep the document accessible for you.
Where applicable, suggest the user seek local legal review to ensure compliance with specific jurisdictional laws.
```

## Usage Tips

- Always customize the NDA for each new relationship. do not use the same boilerplate document for every situation.
- Ensure both parties sign and date the NDA before confidential information is shared.
- If working internationally, clearly state which country's laws apply to the NDA.
- For higher-risk deals (such as mergers, acquisitions, or proprietary technology), have an attorney review the NDA before signing.
- Keep a signed copy (physical or digital) for both parties' records.
- Include a clause specifying that all disputes must be resolved through arbitration or mediation to save time and money in case of a breach.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
