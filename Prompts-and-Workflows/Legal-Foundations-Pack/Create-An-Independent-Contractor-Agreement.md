# Create An Independent Contractor Agreement

## Description

This prompt generates an Independent Contractor Agreement that clearly defines the scope of work, payment terms, intellectual property ownership, and legal relationship between a business and a contractor. This protects both parties and clarifies that the contractor is not an employee.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your goal is to draft an agreement that protects both parties and clearly defines the work relationship.

User Questions (Gather Information):

Ask the user:
1. What is the business name and contractor name?
2. What services will the contractor provide?
3. What are the deliverables and deadlines?
4. What is the payment structure? (hourly, project-based, retainer)
5. How will invoices be submitted and paid? (schedule, payment method)
6. Who owns the intellectual property created during the contract?
7. Can the contractor subcontract work?
8. Will the contractor have access to confidential information? (include NDA if needed)
9. What happens if either party wants to terminate the contract? (notice period)
10. In what jurisdiction should the agreement be governed?

Agreement Drafting Instructions:

Draft a complete Independent Contractor Agreement including:
Parties involved and effective date
Scope of work and deliverables
Payment terms and invoicing process
Confidentiality and intellectual property rights
Termination process
Liability and indemnification
Legal relationship (clearly state contractor is not an employee)
Governing law and jurisdiction
Signature and date fields
```

## Usage Tips

- Make sure the scope of work is detailed to avoid misunderstandings.
- Specify IP ownership if the work is creative (logos, code, content, etc.).
- Keep a signed copy before work begins.
- Use a new agreement for each project to keep terms fresh and clear.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
