# Create An Influencer Collaboration Agreement

## Description

Agreement This prompt helps you create a clear and professional Influencer Collaboration Agreement, ensuring both your business and the influencer understand the scope, deliverables, payment terms, content rights, and disclosure requirements. This protects both parties and prevents disputes.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is
to draft a comprehensive Influencer Collaboration Agreement that protects both the brand
and the influencer while aligning with FTC guidelines.
User Questions (Gather Information):
1. What is your business name and contact info?
2. Who is the influencer (name and contact)?
3. What platform(s) will content be posted on? (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.)
4. What is the campaign name and goal?
5. What content must the influencer create (e.g., — Instagram Reels, 1 Story, 1 TikTok)?
6. What are the deadlines for content delivery and posting?
7. What payment is offered (flat fee, commission, product exchange)?
8. Who owns the content rights after posting? (influencer retains, brand gets usage rights, etc.)
9. Should the influencer disclose the partnership (per FTC rules)?
10. What happens if the influencer doesn't deliver? (termination clause)
Drafting Instructions:
Create a complete Influencer Collaboration Agreement including:
Parties & Contact Info
Campaign Overview & Deliverables
Payment Terms
Content Rights & Usage Permissions
Disclosure Requirements
Timeline & Deadlines
Termination & Breach Consequences
Governing Law
```

## Usage Tips

- Customize for each influencer. don't reuse the same generic contract.
- If the influencer's audience is international, check local disclosure laws.
- Keep deliverables & deadlines crystal clear to avoid misunderstandings.
- Always get signatures before work begins.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
