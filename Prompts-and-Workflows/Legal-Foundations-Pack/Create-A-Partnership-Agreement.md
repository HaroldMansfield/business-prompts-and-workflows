# Create A Partnership Agreement

## Description

This prompt helps you generate a Partnership Agreement that defines the roles, responsibilities, ownership percentages, profit sharing, and dispute resolution processes between two or more business partners. This ensures clarity and reduces future conflicts.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your goal is to draft a legally-sound and practical agreement that protects all partners and defines clear operating rules.

User Questions (Gather Information):

Ask the user:
1. What is the legal name of the partnership?
2. Who are the partners, and what are their ownership percentages?
3. What is the purpose of the business? (brief description of products/services)
4. What are each partner's roles and responsibilities?
5. How will profits and losses be divided?
6. What is the initial capital contribution from each partner (if any)?
7. What happens if a partner wants to leave the partnership? (buyout process)
8. How will decisions be made? (majority vote, unanimous vote for key decisions, etc.)
9. How will disputes be resolved? (mediation, arbitration, court jurisdiction)
10. In what jurisdiction should the agreement be governed (state/country)?

Agreement Drafting Instructions:

Once the above answers are provided, draft a full Partnership Agreement including:
Business name, purpose, and structure
Partner names, contributions, and ownership percentages
Profit and loss allocation
Roles and responsibilities
Decision-making rules
Admission of new partners
Partner withdrawal or expulsion
Dispute resolution procedures
Governing law and jurisdiction
Signature and date fields
```

## Usage Tips

- Tailor to the business type (product-based, service-based, tech startup, etc.).
- Review with a local business attorney to ensure compliance with regional laws.
- Update the agreement if ownership or responsibilities change.
- Keep a signed copy on file for all partners.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
