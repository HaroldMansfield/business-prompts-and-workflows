# Understanding Contracts, Policies, and TOS Agreements With NotebookLM

## Description

TOS agreements with NotebookLM This prompt helps you quickly analyze and understand complex legal documents by leveraging NotebookLM's ability to process documents and answer specific questions about their content. Save hours of reading fine print while ensuring you understand your rights and obligations.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Setup Instructions
1. Upload your document as a source in NotebookLM
Supported formats: Google Docs, Google Slides, PDFs, text files, web pages, copied text,
YouTube videos
For web-only content: save/print the page as PDF before uploading
Privacy Tip: Redact personal information (names, addresses, policy numbers) before
uploading
Ask direct questions about the document (NotebookLM already understands its role, so
2. questions can be simple).
Sample Questions for Insurance
Policies
What are the coverage limits in this policy
and which variables affect the final payout
amount?
What is [company]'s timeline obligation for
claim resolution?
Which specific types of damage and loss
scenarios fall within this policy's protection?
What exclusions or exceptions exist that
might invalidate coverage?
How are policy changes, modifications, or
cancellations handled?
Sample Questions for Business
Contracts
Which specific conditions or actions would
constitute a material breach of this
agreement?
What is the governing law and venue
specification for potential disputes?
Which specific rights am I surrendering by
executing this contract?
How does this agreement address dispute
resolution and mediation processes?
What are the intellectual property and data
ownership provisions?
What indemnification and liability limitation
clauses exist?
Under what conditions can either party
terminate this agreement?
Sample Questions for Terms of Service
With how many third-party entities is my personal information permitted to be shared?
What procedures exist for requesting complete data deletion?
How does this agreement define legitimate business purposes regarding account termination
and data retention?
What intellectual property rights am I granting to the company?
What limitations exist on my ability to pursue legal remedies for disputes?
```

## Usage Tips

- NotebookLM can handle up to 50 sources per notebook.
- Each source can contain up to 500,000 words.
- Total capacity is 25 million words across all sources.
- The tool will point you to exact locations in documents where information appears.
- Use this for any complex document you need to understand quickly.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
