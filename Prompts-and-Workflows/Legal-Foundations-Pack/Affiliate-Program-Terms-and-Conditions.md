# Affiliate Program Terms and Conditions

## Description

This workflow generates a detailed set of Affiliate Program Terms & Conditions that outline the rules, responsibilities, and expectations for affiliates promoting your products or services. It covers commissions, payouts, promotional guidelines, restrictions, and legal disclaimers to ensure your program operates legally and fairly.

## Best Use Cases

- Creating a structured first draft before professional review.
- Gathering the business details needed for a policy, agreement, or compliance document.
- Helping a founder or operator understand what questions need to be answered before finalizing the document.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Your task is to help me draft Affiliate Program Terms & Conditions for my business. Please ask me the following questions one at a time. Once I provide the answers, create a full agreement that I can post on my website or affiliate portal.
1. What is your business name and website URL?
2. What products or services will affiliates be promoting?
3. What is the commission structure? (e.g., % per sale, flat rate, recurring commissions)
4. What is the payout process? (e.g., monthly via PayPal, minimum payout threshold)
5. What tracking system or platform will you use? (e.g., Refersion, Tapfiliate, custom dashboard)
6. Are there any restrictions on how affiliates can promote your products? (e.g., no paid search ads, no email spamming, no coupon sites)
7. Will affiliates have access to marketing assets or approved messaging?
8. How long do cookies or tracking links remain valid? (e.g., 30 days, 90 days)
9. Are there geographic or industry restrictions for affiliates?
10. What actions can result in termination from the program?
11. Do you offer performance incentives or tiers? (optional)
12. Do affiliates need to disclose their relationship per FTC guidelines?
13. In what jurisdiction should these terms be governed?
Once complete, write clear and professional Affiliate Program Terms & Conditions that include:
- Program overview and purpose
- Eligibility and registration terms
- Commission and payout structure
- Tracking, cookies, and attribution policy
- Acceptable and prohibited promotional methods
- Termination and fraud prevention clauses
- FTC disclosure requirements
- Rights to modify program terms
- Dispute resolution and governing law
- Acknowledgment or acceptance language (checkbox or agreement link)
Make the content legally compliant, plain-language where possible, and easy to copy into a website terms page.
```

## Usage Tips

- Essential if you run a public or private affiliate program. especially with automated payouts.
- Include clear promotional restrictions to prevent brand damage or black-hat marketing.
- Add this to your affiliate signup page and require agreement before participation.
- Update the terms if commission structures, policies, or platforms change.
- Include links to resources like your brand guidelines or FTC disclosure requirements.

## Review Notes

Have the final document reviewed by a qualified professional before using it. Confirm that it matches your jurisdiction, business model, customer relationships, and any industry-specific requirements.
