# AI-Powered Market Research Framework

## Description

Framework This framework helps you generate comprehensive market intelligence reports covering trends, competition, consumer behavior, innovations, and growth opportunities for any industry or product category.

## Best Use Cases

- Early-stage business planning and validation.
- Clarifying audience, positioning, offer, or research direction before execution.
- Creating a practical starting point for founder decisions.

## Workflow Prompt

```text
Setup Requirements
Perplexity AI account (recommended for comprehensive research).
NotebookLM for analysis and synthesis.
Both tools open simultaneously for seamless information transfer.
Prompt 1: Data Collection with Perplexity
Function as an expert market intelligence analyst. I need a comprehensive market landscape
assessment for [insert product/service/industry].
Please structure your research to cover these critical dimensions:
Market Dynamics and Quantification:
Current market valuation, growth trajectory, and year-over-year changes
Regional market distribution and geographic expansion patterns
Demand drivers and market evolution catalysts
Seasonality patterns and cyclical influences
Competitive Ecosystem Analysis:
Market concentration and key player identification with estimated market share
Strategic positioning matrix of primary competitors
Distinctive competitive advantages and vulnerability assessment
Recent strategic moves including acquisitions, partnerships, and pivots
Consumer Engagement Patterns:
Demographic and psychographic profiles of primary consumer segments
Purchase journey mapping and decision factors
Shifting loyalty dynamics and brand perception metrics
Emerging consumer behavior trends and preference evolution
Innovation Landscape:
Technological disruptions and their adoption curves
Product development trajectories and feature evolution
Business model innovations changing industry dynamics
Investment patterns and startup ecosystem activity
Research and Scientific Developments:
Synthesis of recent peer-reviewed findings with industry implications
Emerging research directions and academic-industry collaboration instances
Data-backed insights challenging conventional industry wisdom
Regulatory Framework:
Current compliance requirements and enforcement patterns
Pending legislation with potential market impact
International regulatory comparison and harmonization efforts
Industry self-regulation initiatives and standards development
Strategic Opportunity Assessment:
Unmet market needs and gap analysis
Potential market entry points and blue ocean opportunities
Critical risk factors and mitigation approaches
Long-term sustainability considerations and adaptation requirements
Please prioritize data from authoritative industry reports, peer-reviewed research, government
databases, and verified industry expert insights. For each section, include specific data points
with temporal context and source credibility indicators. Where conflicting data exists, present
multiple perspectives with evaluative context.
Prompt 2: Analysis with NotebookLM
Create a new notebook in NotebookLM and add your vetted source
URLs from Perplexity. Then use this prompt:
As a strategic market intelligence consultant, analyze these imported sources on [Topic] and
synthesize:
Strategic Insight Extraction:
Identify 5 recurring strategic themes across sources with supporting evidence
Pinpoint contradictions or competing perspectives within the industry discourse
Map interconnections between seemingly distinct market developments
Highlight counterintuitive findings that challenge prevailing industry assumptions
Market Evolution Analysis:
Construct a timeline of critical industry inflection points from the data
Identify early indicators of emerging market shifts
Evaluate the maturity stage of key technologies or business models
Assess disruption vulnerability in established market segments
Competitive Landscape Assessment:
Map the strategic positioning of key players based on differentiation approaches
Identify capability gaps and potential strategic partnership opportunities
Evaluate effectiveness of different business models within the ecosystem
Highlight underserved market segments and potential whitespace opportunities
Actionable Intelligence Framework:
Formulate 5-7 key strategic questions requiring further investigation
Identify specific metrics to track for early detection of market shifts
Outline potential scenario planning frameworks based on key uncertainties
Suggest modified research methodologies to address information gaps
Present your analysis with clearly with evidence-based reasoning, and strategic implications for
decision-makers.
```

## Usage Tips

- Answer the prompt questions with specific details from your business.
- Treat the AI output as a working draft and revise it before using it.
- Save the final version somewhere your team can find and reuse it.

## Review Notes

Review the output carefully, adapt it to your business context, and verify important facts before sharing or using it.
